## IconMCP

The standard UI icons in `apps/web/src/app/icons.tsx` are vendored from [IconMCP](https://iconmcp.com/), using its outline style with a 16-unit grid, 1.33 stroke and radius 2. Each component names its source icon. These SVGs are bundled locally; the app does not connect to IconMCP. [Source licence](https://iconmcp.com/licence).
