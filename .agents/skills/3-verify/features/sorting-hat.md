# Sorting Hat

A hub can run its threads through a Sorting Hat of its own (ADR 0018): Settings / Hubs takes its address and an admin token pasted from its dashboard, each profile links to one of its houses, and a thread gets a hat key for its profile's house and its machine, plus the Sorting Hat's address. Account settings, profile account selection, limits and the account in use then go through its management API. Without one, the hub keeps accounts itself, as before; connecting moves them there once (`apps/hub/src/accountMove.ts`, covered by `accountMove.test.ts`, not driven in the browser), and a failed move shows "This hub’s accounts could not be moved to the Sorting Hat yet: …" in the card's alert. The admin token stays on the hub: no client, protocol message or thread receives it.

Run `node .agents/skills/3-verify/scripts/verify.mjs .agents/skills/3-verify/scripts/sorting-hat.mjs` after building. The journey starts the Sorting Hat fixture (`scripts/sortinghat-test-server.ts`: a standalone Sorting Hat with faked providers, every Claude account reporting the same limits) and stands in for its dashboard through the admin API: houses `Personal` (with a signed-out `Dashboard Claude`) and `Work`. Provider consent pages are routed to a stub; no real provider is contacted and no turn is sent.

Mapped entry points:

- `sorting-hat/settings`: Settings / Hubs → card `Sorting Hat` → textboxes `Address`, `Admin token` (a password field; use `getByLabel`), `This hub’s name on the Sorting Hat` (prefilled with the saved hub's name) → `Connect Sorting Hat`. A wrong token must show "The Sorting Hat refused the admin token…" in the card's alert. Connected, the card shows the host, `<n> houses` and "Hat keys are named “Accio · <name> · …”." with `Change` and `Disconnect`; `Disconnect` asks first ("Threads go back to the accounts this hub keeps itself…"), then deletes the hub's hat keys on the Sorting Hat. The page and `<home>/hub/*` must not contain the admin token after saving.
- `sorting-hat/accounts`: Settings / Accounts reads "… Accounts are kept on the Sorting Hat at <host>." and works as in [Accounts](accounts.md): `Add Claude account`, `Sign in with Claude` (the fixture's consent page redirects through the worker's loopback callback, as in `tests/limits.spec.ts`), rename, delete; each lands on the Sorting Hat. A dashboard change shows within 15 seconds. Deleting an account that another house holds shows "This account is still in the house <house> on the Sorting Hat. …".
- `sorting-hat/profiles`: a profile without a house reads `<name> No house on the Sorting Hat` in Settings / Profiles. Its form has the MenuSelect `House on the Sorting Hat`; picking another house hides the account lists until Save ("Save to use the accounts of <house>."). Saved, the row reads `<name> House <house> · <accounts>` and the account lists show the house's accounts; choosing and ordering them sets the house's accounts on the Sorting Hat, for every app using it (the hint says so). `Add profile` preselects `New house <name>` ("Save to make this house on the Sorting Hat."); the other options are the existing houses. First-run setup links the house of the profile's name, or makes it. `profiles` in `<home>/hub/settings.json` carries `houseId`.
- `sorting-hat/limits`: the Limits view and the composer's limits chip show the Sorting Hat's limits and account in use; `Refresh` asks it to read them again.
- `sorting-hat/threads`: a draft or thread of a profile without a house shows "<name> isn’t linked to a house on the Sorting Hat, so this thread can’t run yet." with `Choose house`, which opens that profile; Send stays disabled. Starting a turn anyway (RPC) answers the same message.
- `sorting-hat/unreachable`: within 15 seconds of the Sorting Hat stopping, Settings / Accounts and the profile form show "Could not reach the Sorting Hat at <host>. …" in place of their lists, the Limits view shows it as a banner, and the composer chip reads `Sorting Hat unreachable`.

The journey covers all six, signing in one Claude account. ChatGPT sign-in through the hub, the pasted Claude code and limits refresh are covered by `apps/hub/src/sortingHatLink.test.ts`, as are hat-key naming, reuse across restarts, unpairing and disconnecting; first-run setup on a hub that already has a Sorting Hat is not driven. A real turn through a Sorting Hat is not part of it; `apps/worker/src/threads/sortingHat.integration.test.ts` runs Claude Code and Codex turns through a standalone Sorting Hat with faked providers. Report live-provider sign-ins and turns through a deployed Sorting Hat as unverified unless driven.
